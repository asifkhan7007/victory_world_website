01

Introduction

Victory World is an independent gaming platform being developed by INRICA. References to “Victory World,” “we,” “our,” or “us” in this policy refer to the Victory World website, app, and related services.

The platform is currently under development. Some services described in this policy may not yet be available. We will update this policy as features, data practices, and legal availability change.

02

Information We May Collect

The information collected will depend on which Victory World features you use.

Information you provide

  • Account details such as a name, username, email address, telephone number, password, or selected avatar.
  • Messages and information submitted through customer support, feedback, or contact forms.
  • Age, date-of-birth, location, or identity-verification information when legally required for a particular service.
  • Payment, deposit, withdrawal, or transaction information if real-money features are introduced.
  • Preferences, game selections, room invitations, and other information you choose to provide.

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system, browser type, language, app version, and device identifiers.
  • IP address, approximate location, time zone, and connection information.
  • Website activity, pages viewed, feature interactions, crashes, diagnostics, and performance information.
  • Game activity such as rooms joined, actions taken, results, connection status, and gameplay history.
Current website notice

This promotional website does not currently provide player registration, deposits, withdrawals, or real-money gameplay. This policy will be updated before those services are made publicly available.

03

How We May Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create, maintain, authenticate, and secure player accounts.
  • Provide games, multiplayer rooms, matchmaking, avatars, rewards, support, and platform functionality.
  • Process transactions and maintain legally required records if payment features are introduced.
  • Confirm age, identity, location, eligibility, or compliance where required by applicable law.
  • Detect fraud, cheating, misuse, security threats, and violations of platform rules.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve performance, reliability, usability, and game design.
  • Communicate service updates, security notices, support responses, and important policy changes.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.
04

How We May Share Information

We do not intend to sell personal information for money. Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary, including to:

  • Hosting, cloud, communications, security, analytics, customer support, and payment-service providers.
  • Identity, age, location, fraud-prevention, or regulatory verification providers when required.
  • Professional advisers such as attorneys, accountants, auditors, or insurers.
  • Government authorities or law-enforcement agencies in response to valid legal requirements.
  • A successor organization in connection with a merger, restructuring, financing, sale, or transfer of the business.

Other players may see limited profile information such as your display name, avatar, seat, game actions, and public results while participating in multiplayer features.

05

Cookies and Analytics

Victory World may use cookies, local storage, software development kits, or similar technologies to keep services functional, remember preferences, protect accounts, measure performance, and understand how the platform is used.

Where legally required, users will be given appropriate choices before non-essential analytics or advertising technologies are used.

06

Data Retention

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining security, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and meeting legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the feature involved, and applicable legal requirements.

07

Information Security

We intend to use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed.

No internet service, application, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Players are responsible for protecting their passwords, devices, and account-access information.

08

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include the ability to:

  • Request access to information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Request a portable copy of certain information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Opt out of certain sharing, targeted advertising, or promotional communications where applicable.
  • Appeal certain decisions concerning a privacy request where applicable law provides that right.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Some requests may be limited or denied where permitted or required by law.

Age Restrictions and Children’s Privacy

Victory World is intended only for adults who meet the minimum legal age required for the relevant service in their jurisdiction. The platform is not directed to children.

We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If we learn that information was collected from a person who was not legally permitted to use the service, we may delete the information and restrict the associated account.

10

International Use and Transfers

Victory World may be accessed from multiple countries. Where legally permitted, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where it was collected.

Applicable safeguards will be considered when transferring personal information across national borders.

11

Third-Party Services and Links

Victory World may link to app stores, social networks, payment providers, sports-data providers, or other third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy.

Review the applicable third-party terms and privacy policies before providing information to those services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Victory World develops, introduces new features, changes service providers, or responds to legal and regulatory requirements.

The updated policy will display a revised “Last updated” date. Additional notice may be provided when a material change requires it.
