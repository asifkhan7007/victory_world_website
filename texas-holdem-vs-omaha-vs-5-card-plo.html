UNDERSTAND THE FORMATS

The Three Victory World Variants

Texas Hold'em

Each player receives two hole cards. Build the strongest five-card hand from the seven available cards. You may use both, one or none of your hole cards if the community board itself is best.

Good for: New Poker players and anyone who prefers simpler starting decisions.

Texas Hold'em guide

Omaha

Each player receives four hole cards, but a valid hand must use exactly two of them together with exactly three community cards. More combinations create stronger draws and made hands.

Good for: Players ready to evaluate connected cards, nut potential and redraws.

Omaha guide

5 Card PLO

Each player receives five hole cards and still must use exactly two with exactly three community cards. The extra card creates even more possible draws and overlapping combinations.

Good for: Experienced Omaha players who enjoy highly connected hands and complex draw structures.

5 Card PLO guide

FIND YOUR GAME

Which Poker Variant Should You Choose?

Start with Texas Hold'em if...

  • You are learning poker hand rankings and betting rounds.
  • You want fewer starting-card combinations to evaluate.
  • You want to build a foundation in position, folding and board reading.

Move to Omaha if...

  • You understand Hold'em and want more draw possibilities.
  • You are comfortable remembering the exact two-hole-card rule.
  • You want to study nut hands, coordinated cards and redraws.

Try 5 Card PLO if...

  • You already understand standard Omaha hand construction.
  • You enjoy evaluating several simultaneous draws.
  • You can fold attractive-looking hands that do not draw to the nuts.

AVOID THESE

Common Poker Variant Mistakes

  • Using one Omaha hole card: Omaha and 5 Card PLO always require exactly two.
  • Using three Omaha hole cards: A valid hand can never use more than two.
  • Overvaluing small flushes: Extra hole-card combinations make stronger flushes and redraws more common in Omaha formats.
  • Playing every connected hand: More possibilities do not make every starting hand profitable or strong.
  • Ignoring the board: Always identify the strongest possible five-card hands and how the turn or river can change them.
  • Forgetting the timer: Choose the available 30-second or 70-second turn time that fits your experience.

SAME HAND ORDER

Poker Hand Rankings

All three variants use the same standard five-card hand order:

  1. Royal Flush
  2. Straight Flush
  3. Four of a Kind
  4. Full House
  5. Flush
  6. Straight
  7. Three of a Kind
  8. Two Pair
  9. One Pair
  10. High Card

The difference is not the ranking order; it is which cards each variant permits you to use.

QUICK ANSWERS

Poker Variants FAQ

Which Poker variant is best for beginners?

Texas Hold'em is usually the easiest starting point because each player receives only two hole cards and has fewer combinations to evaluate.

How many hole cards are dealt?

Texas Hold'em deals two, Omaha deals four, and 5 Card PLO deals five.

Do I have to use exactly two hole cards in Omaha?

Yes. Both Omaha and 5 Card PLO require exactly two hole cards and exactly three community cards.

Must I use both hole cards in Texas Hold'em?

No. You use the strongest five-card combination available and may use both, one or none of your hole cards.

Do all three games use the same hand rankings?

Yes. Standard five-card poker hand rankings apply to all three variants.
