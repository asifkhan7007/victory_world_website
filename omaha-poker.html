How Omaha works

Each player receives four hole cards. To make a valid five-card hand, you must use exactly two hole cards and exactly three community cards.

Omaha strategy

More hole-card combinations create stronger draws and bigger hands than Texas Hold'em, so coordinated cards and redraws matter.

Play modes

Practice against AI or play Omaha in supported private and public multiplayer rooms using free virtual chips.
